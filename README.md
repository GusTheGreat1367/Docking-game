### STRATA ORBITAL REMOTE OVERRIDE OFFICER DESK
This desk in strata orbital is in charge of docking all delivery vehicles to a space station and handling any issues after launch when in Low Earth Orbit.

###DOCKING
  When docking in Strata Orbital, you have a random starting location and amount of fuel, because no launch is the same. 
  The primary objective is to safely dock to the station, but sometimes there is an error, like no main thruster or RCS down.
  Catastrophic errors have a 1% chance of happening, but when they do it destroys the vehicle, a component of it, or renders the craft uncontrollable;
  like when you activate the throttle it explodes or when rolling it locks and continues and cannot be stopped. 
  Errors can become catastrophic if you continue to use the component. 
  EX: RCS down has an error and cannot work, but if you continue trying to use it, it will turn catastrophic and run until you are out of fuel (or it is deactivated).
  The control panel can be used to deactivate components when they have an error.
